Lyric Tap Sync

Time a song's lyrics by tapping along. Your audio stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

1 · Song and lyrics

Paste the lyrics, one sung line per line. Empty lines and [Section] tags are ignored.

2 · Tap

Load a song and paste the lyrics
0:00.00

Lines: tap the instant each line starts being sung. Words: tap the instant each word starts (do this after the lines are done; slower playback helps). Keys: Space tap · Backspace undo · R resume before last tap · P play/pause.

3 · Review and nudge

Click a time to play from just before it; nudge by 50 ms.

4 · Export

Send the .json back in the chat. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
